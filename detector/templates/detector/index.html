About SkinSense

SkinSense uses a CNN (Convolutional Neural Network) model trained on the HAM10000 dataset to detect and classify skin lesion images into seven different types of skin diseases.

  • akiec – Actinic keratoses
  • bcc – Basal cell carcinoma
  • bkl – Benign keratosis-like lesions
  • df – Dermatofibroma
  • mel – Melanoma
  • nv – Melanocytic nevi
  • vasc – Vascular lesions

The model improves over time as more images are trained, ensuring more accurate and reliable predictions.
